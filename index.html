Kenya Safari Packages & Maasai Mara Wildlife Tours

VoyageMara Safaris is a locally owned Kenyan tour operator based in Nairobi, specializing in custom private 4x4 Land Cruiser safaris, certified native Maasai guides, and small-group journeys across Maasai Mara National Reserve, Amboseli National Park, Samburu, Lake Nakuru, and Lake Naivasha.

Featured Kenya Safari Destinations

Maasai Mara National Reserve

The crown jewel of African wildlife. Experience the Great Wildebeest Migration, resident lion prides, cheetah hunts, and luxury tented camps along the Mara River.

Amboseli National Park

Iconic free-ranging elephant herds roaming beneath Mount Kilimanjaro with panoramic savannah wetlands.

Samburu National Reserve

Rugged northern wilderness sanctuary home to the rare Special Five: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, Somali ostrich, and beisa oryx.

Lake Nakuru National Park

Protected black and white rhino sanctuary on the floor of the Great Rift Valley, with Rothschild giraffes and seasonal birdlife.

Lake Naivasha & Crescent Island

Tranquil freshwater boat safaris past wild hippos and guided walking safaris alongside zebras and giraffes.
